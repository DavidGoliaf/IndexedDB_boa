## Notes

*F3 (JS↔core):* total 21.5 µs includes the 6.8 µs completion-counter
readback (separately measured `eval_readback` id); production requests do
not pay it. Derived true cost ≈ 14.7 µs. Consistent across three runs
(20.7/21.5/21.5 µs totals); one loaded run showed 43 µs total (queue lag
under rapid fire — the drain-until-done design absorbs it honestly rather
than measuring uncompleted round trips). Margin is thin (~2 %); the
remainder is Boa job-queue/event-dispatch mechanics, out of backend scope.
*F5 (FS strict):* 212 → 236/s across runs (fsync floor ~4.6 ms/commit on
this host); variance is environmental, watched by the nightly canary.
*20k diagnostic scans* (criterion `cursor_scan_20000` ids): memory 5.6M/s,
SQLite 3.7M/s, FS 4.7M/s — not evidence, rate references only.
